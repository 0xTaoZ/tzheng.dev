At first this site was only a simple portfolio. But I started to think of it like a small product.

The site should be fast, readable, and easy to update. It should show projects, notes, and learning without feeling too heavy.

I also like that frontend has engineering inside it. Animation, performance, accessibility, layout, and state all need decisions.

My goal is not to make the site perfect in one day. My goal is to keep improving it like a real system.
