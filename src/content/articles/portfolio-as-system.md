I started this portfolio as a custom homepage with a dark interface, a terminal and animated details. As I added projects and notes, putting all the information inside that page became harder to maintain.

The next step was to give articles and projects their own records and URLs while keeping the homepage's visual character. That meant working on content structure and publishing as well as the interface.

This is an early note about that direction. The [Content Collections note](/articles/astro-content-system-notes/) describes how the records are used now, and [System Design](/system/) documents the current implementation.
